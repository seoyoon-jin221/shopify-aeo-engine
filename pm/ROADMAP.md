## Milestone Schedule

| Sprint | Timeline | Theme | Key Deliverables |

| **Sprint 0** | Day 1 (2 Hours) | **Plumbing & Scaffolding** | Shopify Partner setup, Dev Store creation, Monorepo initialization |

| **Sprint 2** | Days 8–14 | **Embedded UI & Storefront** | Polaris audit scorecard, 1-Click Fix action, Liquid theme app blocks |
| **Sprint 3** | Days 15–21 | **Billing & Submission** | Shopify Recurring Subscriptions, GDPR webhooks, App Store asset submission |
| **Sprint 4** | Days 22–28 | **Distribution Flywheel** | Cold audit outreach to 50 indie brands, first 10 installs, 5 verified 5-star reviews |
