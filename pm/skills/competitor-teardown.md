# Skill: Competitor Tear-down & Feature Parity Matrix
> Adapted from `assimovt/productskills`.

## Purpose
Enables an agent to dissect competing products across capability, pricing, moat, and market vulnerabilities.

## Framework
1. **Target Competitors**: Identify 3 direct competitors and 2 adjacent players.
2. **Feature Parity Matrix**:
   - Must-have parity features (table stakes)
   - Differentiators (unique value prop)
   - Competitor weaknesses / unmet customer complaints
3. **Pricing & Packaging Breakdown**: Free tier limits, expansion triggers, price per seat/unit.
4. **Counter-Positioning Strategy**: How we win without directly attacking their strength.
